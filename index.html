Sarasota County Census Tract Demographics (2020)

Demographic Distribution Across Sarasota County Census Tracts

This map displays racial and ethnic demographics across Sarasota County census tracts from the 2020 Decennial Census:

  • Hispanic: Higher concentrations in north Sarasota and central tracts.
  • White (non-Hispanic): Makes up the majority across coastal and suburban tracts.
  • Black: Highest shares in Newtown and northern city neighborhoods.
  • Asian: Distributed across suburban tracts, including Lakewood Ranch and Palmer Ranch.

Use the radio buttons in the upper right to switch between demographic groups. Hover over any census tract to inspect all four percentages.
